I'm Adit, an undergraduate at UC Berkeley studying Electrical Engineering and Computer Science.

I'm interested in building efficient models of sensory processing and how learning mechanisms leverage the inherent structure in natural data. I also work on creative machine learning projects with Launchpad and I'm a director of Health Engine, an accelerator for early-stage healthcare startups.
